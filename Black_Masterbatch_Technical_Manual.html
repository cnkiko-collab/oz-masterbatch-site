3. Processing Guide

Film Blowing

Prioritize high-dispersion series with low filter pressure rise. Control moisture and avoid excessive shear heat.

Pipe Extrusion

Use PE/HDPE carrier resin and UV/weather resistant carbon black for outdoor pipe and fittings.

Injection Molding

PP or ABS carrier should match base resin. Check MFI compatibility to avoid flow marks or surface defects.

Recycled Plastics

Use stronger tinting strength and stable dispersion to correct color fluctuation in recycled resin batches.

4. Quality Control Checklist

  • MFI compatibility with customer base resin.
  • Carbon black dispersion and filter pressure test.
  • Heat resistance under actual processing temperature.
  • Color strength and ΔE consistency between batches.
  • Compliance documents: RoHS, REACH, MSDS, and requested food-contact statement if applicable.

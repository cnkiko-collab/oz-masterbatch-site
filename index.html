FAQ for Buyers

Common Black Masterbatch Questions

What is the difference between carbon black and black masterbatch?

Carbon black is a pigment powder. Black masterbatch is carbon black dispersed into a carrier resin, making it easier and cleaner for plastic processors to dose and process.

How much black masterbatch should be added?

Typical dosage is 1%-6%. Thin film, outdoor pipe and high opacity products usually need higher dosage than thick injection molded parts.

Why does black masterbatch cause black spots?

Common reasons include poor carbon black dispersion, wrong carrier resin, moisture, excessive dosage, low processing temperature or screen pack issues.

Which carrier should I choose?

Choose a carrier compatible with the base resin. PP products usually need PP carrier; PE/HDPE film and pipe usually need PE or HDPE carrier.

Can you support outdoor UV applications?

Yes. We can recommend UV resistant black masterbatch for mulch film, geomembrane, outdoor pipe and products exposed to sunlight.

Can I request a TDS before ordering?

Yes. Send application, resin and target performance; we can provide a suitable grade recommendation and TDS.
